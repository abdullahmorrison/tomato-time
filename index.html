Tomato Time

Let your chat throw tomatoes at you. No download, no login, no account to connect.

The Twitch channel whose chat the overlay listens to.

2 · Options

Fine if you leave these alone.

3 · Your link

Copy this, then follow the steps below.

4 · Add it to OBS

  1. In OBS, under Sources, click + and choose Browser.
  2. Give it a name (Tomato Time) and click OK.
  3. Paste your link into the URL box.
  4. Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080.
  5. Click OK. Drag the layer to the top of your source list so tomatoes land in front of everything.

To start a round, you or one of your mods types !tomato in chat. For the next 30 seconds anyone typing tomatoTime throws a tomato. Only mods and you can start a round — regular chatters can't.
